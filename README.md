# Lemonada Galactica
This is my submission for a college course finals project [the task was to create an interactive adobe animate piece with at least 3 buttons].

Once, a meteor fell into a boys backyard. He went to go check and learned the meteor was fragile; he could break parts of it off! He slowly tore pieces off and one day he saw it reacted to the lemonade. With the meteor bits, he was able to expand his business... at a cost.

This is just a simple clicker game, have fun!
